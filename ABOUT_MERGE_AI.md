# About Merge AI

**Creator and project owner:** Khalid Bin Ashraf  
**Original purpose:** Seventh-grade science fair project  
**Official app:** https://mergeai.streamlit.app/

## Why I created Merge AI

I created Merge AI to explore how multiple artificial intelligence providers can work together to give one useful answer. I wanted an easy-to-use assistant that could help people with research and everyday questions. This science fair project also let me practice programming, testing, and publishing a real web application.

## What it does

Merge AI combines services including Gemini, Groq, and OpenRouter. Its Streamlit interface offers Google sign-in, account-based chat history via Supabase, web search, and optional account memory. Memory suggestions are not saved automatically: each user must approve what is stored.

## Privacy

Khalid is the creator of the application, not the owner of users' private memories. Every signed-in account has its own chat history and approved saved memories. The model receives relevant approved memories when that feature is enabled.
